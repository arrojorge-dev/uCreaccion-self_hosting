# uCreacción

The new name puts the focus where the real value of the product lies:

## You create.

**uCreacción** combines the idea of **“you”** with **“creation.”**

Because AI should not replace your ability to create.

It should help you turn the idea in your head into something you can actually use, share, or deliver.

**You have the idea.  
uCreacción helps turn it into reality.**

---

## From Prompt Generator to Creation Platform

The name TPG was closely tied to one specific part of the technology: the **Prompt Generator**.

It was the right name to explain how the project started.

But it limited the way people perceived the product.

Today, uCreacción goes much further:

**Idea → Understanding → Refinement → Master Prompt → Generation → Result**

The prompt is still a fundamental part of the process.

But **it is no longer the final product**.

It is the mechanism that transforms human intent into something useful.

---

## A brand built for the next stage of AI

Artificial intelligence is evolving rapidly.

More models, providers, and tools are appearing every day.

That is why uCreacción does not want to define itself by a specific model or a single technology.

The brand represents a broader idea:

> **Making AI-powered creation accessible to everyone, regardless of their technical expertise.**
